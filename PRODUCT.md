# Product
<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
Web, using buildless HTML, CSS, and JavaScript.

## Users
People researching Binance USDT perpetual futures, including beginners who need plain explanations.

## Product Purpose
Let a visitor enter a contract and holding horizon, review a rule-based live assessment, inspect conditional long and short scenarios, and estimate position risk.

## Operating Context
The page is public and requires no login. Monitoring runs while the page is open and visible. Exchange availability and internet connectivity affect results.

## Capabilities and Constraints
Public Binance REST and WebSocket data; completed-candle analysis; fresh-quote and fresh-analysis gates; editable cost assumptions; browser-local calculator inputs. No order placement, exchange credentials, trained prediction model, or claimed win rate. A scenario alone is not an entry signal.

## Brand Commitments
Professional workspace with compact overview, prominent chart, and clear live status, confirmed by the user on November 5, 2026. Keep the optional Beginner switch, light/dark themes, public access, and existing signal rules. Use neutral panel outlines without decorative colored edge stripes.

## Evidence on Hand
Existing live app, source, safety tests, DESIGN.md, the user's screenshots and feedback, and their selected professional workspace direction.

## Product Principles
Show the decision clearly, provide its evidence, and keep conditional plans distinct from confirmed entries. Preserve analysis behavior when changing presentation.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Readable type, visible keyboard focus, labeled controls, text in addition to state colors, reduced motion support, responsive charts, and plain-language Beginner view with a glossary.
