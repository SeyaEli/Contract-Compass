# Workspace composition

Mode: Operate. Code-led refinement of the established Graphite system in DESIGN.md; no new visual identity or generated imagery.

Confirmed direction: Professional workspace — compact overview, prominent chart, clear live status. Preserve Beginner view, both themes, public access, and existing signal rules.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Put contract selection, fresh status, the decision, and its price context in one scan before the evidence and risk tools.

OWN-WORLD: Inherit Graphite light/dark tokens, Segoe UI, tabular values, blue/orange candle encoding, neutral one-pixel outlines, and restrained controls. No decorative border stripes or outlined signal-code badges.

STORY: Choose a contract, read its live status and decision, inspect the chart and checks, then review conditional levels and position risk.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Compact heading with Beginner switch, one search area, monitoring controls, and combined market/decision overview leading into the dominant chart. No marketing hero.

FORM: User-pinned professional workspace composition. Labeled desktop navigation, responsive overview, dominant chart with adjacent checks, compact scenario levels and calculator, and secondary history/methodology. The user selected the direction directly; no open visual-world tournament or comp-led build is involved.

## Responsive and state contract

Desktop has labeled navigation when space allows. Narrower layouts use an icon rail; phones use a top navigation row. The chart fits its container with readable labels and shows 30 recent candles below 500px versus 70 at larger sizes. Indicator calculations and entry rules always use the same full input history. Hidden Beginner details never suppress the primary decision. Loading, error, pause, reconnect, and stale-data states remain explicit.

## Quality bar

Operate precision: aligned values and controls, readable supporting text, coherent spacing, native form behavior, visible focus, text-labeled states, and consistent authored SVG icons. No decorative motion or unrelated dashboard metrics. The chart is functional evidence, not illustration.
